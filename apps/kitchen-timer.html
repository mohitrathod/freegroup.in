Kitchen Timer

A simple, offline countdown timer for the kitchen.

Kitchen Timer lets you choose a duration, start or pause a countdown, and stop the alarm when time is up. It works locally on your Android device and does not require an account.

Privacy policy

Information we collect

Kitchen Timer does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data-collection services.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your last selected timer duration locally on your device so it can restore that preference. This information is not sent to FreeGroup or any third party.

Permissions

Kitchen Timer may use notifications, an exact alarm, a foreground timer service, and a short-lived wake lock to keep the timer accurate and play the alarm when the countdown finishes. These capabilities are used only for the timer experience.

Children's privacy

Kitchen Timer does not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
