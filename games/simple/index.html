Mini Games Collection

30 simple offline games. Open any card to play — no installation, no internet needed.

🐍 Snake Classic snake. Eat, grow, don't hit the walls or yourself. 🧭 Tetris Stack falling blocks and clear lines. Hold, next, hard drop. ⭕ Tic-Tac-Toe Beat the AI or play a friend on the same device. 🃏 Memory Match Flip cards and find all matching pairs in the fewest moves. 🎳 Breakout Break every brick with the ball. Levels ramp up fast. 🔢 2048 Merge tiles up to 2048 and beyond. Swipe or arrow keys. 🐥 Flappy Bird Tap to flap through the pipes. How far can you go? 💣 Minesweeper Clear the board without detonating any mines. Three sizes. ✎️ Rock Paper Scissors Best-of battles against a cunning computer. 🎵 Simon Says Repeat the growing light-and-sound sequence. Test your memory. 🏓 Pong The original paddle duel. Play the AI or a friend. 👾 Space Invaders Blast the descending alien wave before it reaches you. 🟠 Connect Four Drop discs and line up four in a row against the AI. 🐢 Whack-a-Mole Tap the moles as fast as they pop up. Beat the clock. 🔐 Hangman Guess the hidden word letter by letter before you run out. 🔥 Sliding Puzzle Slide the tiles back into order. Classic 15-puzzle. ⚡ Reaction Test Wait for green, then click. Measure your reflexes. ⌨️ Typing Test Type the passage and track your WPM and accuracy. ☄ Asteroids Fly, rotate and shoot the drifting rocks apart. 🟰 Doodle Jump Bounce ever upward across platforms without falling. 👻 Pac-Man Eat all dots, avoid ghosts. Power pellets turn the tables. 🐸 Frogger Hop across traffic and rivers to reach the goal. 🔴 Bubble Shooter Match 3+ bubbles to pop them and clear the board. 🚩 Maze Escape Navigate procedural mazes and reach the exit fast. 🔍 Word Search Find hidden words in the grid. Horizontal, vertical, diagonal. 💡 Lights Out Toggle lights to turn them all off. Classic puzzle. 🚘 Car Dodge Switch lanes and avoid oncoming traffic at high speed. 🚁 Helicopter Hold to rise, release to fall. Fly through the cave. 🎨 Color Match Stroop test: click the word's color, not the word itself. 🔶 Nonogram Fill the grid using number clues. Classic picross puzzle.
Made with vanilla HTML, CSS & JS. All games run fully offline.
